## 🛂 Visa Requirements for Gulf Citizens (2026)

### Schengen Visa — 27 European Countries
- **Required Documents:** Passport, photos, hotel booking, flight booking, bank statement, travel insurance
- **Cost:** €90 adults / €45 children (6-12) / Free under 6
- **Processing Time:** 15 working days (up to 45 days in summer)
- **Full Guide:** [تأشيرة شنغن للسعوديين](https://safravip.com/tashirat-schengen-lil-saudiyin/)

- 🇬🇪 Georgia — Visa free
